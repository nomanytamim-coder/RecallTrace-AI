
# RecallTrace
### AI-Powered Food Safety & Supply Chain Traceability

**Trace the Source. Detect the Risk. Protect Lives.**

## The Problem

In 2026, a Cyclospora outbreak linked to contaminated iceberg lettuce supplied by Taylor Farms de Mexico affected:

- **12,883 people infected**
- **570 hospitalizations**
- **2 deaths**
- **21 U.S. states affected**

The outbreak included illnesses associated with lettuce served at Taco Bell and products distributed through Walmart.

According to the FDA and CDC, identifying outbreak-related infections can take up to six weeks, making food contamination difficult to trace quickly.

## Why It Matters

Food travels through complex supply chains involving farms, processors, distributors, retailers, and restaurants. When contamination occurs, identifying the source and affected locations can take time, putting thousands of people at risk.

## Our Solution

**RecallTrace** is an AI-powered food safety intelligence platform designed to help investigators trace contaminated food products through complex supply chains using real-world government data.

Our goal is to make food safety investigations faster, more transparent, and accessible.

## How It Works

1. **Real-World Data:** Uses publicly available FDA and CDC outbreak and recall information.
2. **Supply Chain Visualization:** Displays relationships between farms, suppliers, distributors, and retailers.
3. **AI-Powered Analysis:** Helps interpret outbreak reports, identify connections, and summarize findings.
4. **Risk Investigation:** Helps users explore potentially affected products and locations while distinguishing verified information from suspected connections.

## Our Mission

To help investigators identify contamination sources faster, improve food supply chain transparency, and prevent future foodborne outbreaks.
